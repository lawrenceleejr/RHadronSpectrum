## Assessment (written after unblinding, 2026-11-26)

**β = 6.0 (293 configurations) — robust.**  The lightest state at every quark mass is the
**R-glueball (1⁺⁻)**.  The light R-mesons (0⁻ "R-π-like", 1⁻ "R-ρ-like") lie above it:
r0 ΔE ≈ +0.5 (≈ +200 MeV) at m_π ≈ 650–840 MeV, decreasing toward the lightest mass, where the 1⁻
R-meson is +0.19 ± 0.13 r0 (+77 ± 50 MeV) and the 0⁻ +0.44 ± 0.14 r0 (+172 ± 54 MeV) above the
R-glueball.  At the lightest quark mass the 1⁻ R-meson lies below the 0⁻ one (≈1.5σ, uncorrelated
estimate), the sign expected for a colour-octet hyperfine interaction.  Positive-parity R-mesons are
≈ +1.2–1.5 r0 (≈ +500 MeV).  R-baryons are ≈ +1.4–2.5 r0; the lightest R-baryon is the flavour-singlet
uds state (Farrar's S⁰) together with the Λ-like octet, +1.4 ± 0.2 r0 (≈ +550 MeV) at the lightest
mass, i.e. ≈ 1.2 r0 (≈ 470 MeV) above the lightest R-meson.  Strange R-mesons (ss) do not depend on
the light κ (consistency check).  Consistent with Foster–Michael (quenched, vector R-meson − R-glueball
= 47(90) MeV).

**β = 6.2 (89 configurations) — not yet determined.**  Most R-meson fits latch onto late,
noise-dominated windows (t = 12–18) with fit-window systematics ≈ 0.1 in lattice units, giving
unphysical negative splittings (0⁻ at −0.7 to −1.6 r0 with ±0.7–0.8).  These entries are not a
physics result.  The one channel with a sane window (κ = 0.13530, 1⁻, t = 2–13) gives +0.44 ± 0.41 r0,
consistent with β = 6.0.  Needed: the remaining ≈ 290 β = 6.2 configurations (now generated) and a
signal-to-noise cut on t_max (documented as a post-unblinding change; it removes noise-only windows
and cannot move results toward any reference).

**Comparison with the ATLAS model (ATL-PHYS-PUB-2019-019).**  The model's same-set range
Λ̄(g̃g) − Λ̄(g̃qq̄) ∈ [−0.017, +0.297] GeV has the gluinoball at or *above* the R-ρ; at β = 6.0 we find
the R-glueball *below* the R-mesons by ≈ 80–220 MeV (−0.08 to −0.22 GeV), and the R-baryons ≈ 0.5 GeV
above the R-mesons where the model has them degenerate or lighter (R-Λ − R-ρ ∈ [−0.12, 0.00] GeV).
Both differences are large, but these are **quenched, heavy-pion (525–840 MeV), single-spacing**
numbers: R-baryon − R-meson splittings shrink toward the physical point (cf. the nucleon), and
quenching effects on the octet q q̄ cloud are unknown.  Stage B (dynamical, m_π ≈ 570–700 MeV) and
the continuum/chiral extrapolation are required before any phenomenological statement.
